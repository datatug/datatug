## Money, calendar, units and content

Currency unit, ISO alphabetic/numeric representation, fund code and dated territory assignment differ. Country-to-currency is many-to-many over time and cannot establish invoice or salary currency. Employees salaries remain without an established currency/country. Other monetary hypotheses need their own unit evidence. Chinook's existing USD bindings are explicit fixture modelling assumptions; they are not native currency columns or facts inferred from country. Catalogue asking price reuses core `price`; generic catalogue `UnitPrice` → invoice-line `unit-price` is rejected, with a corrected Chinook mapping retained separately.

ECB rates are indicative quote units per EUR, with publication date/provider/rate kind. A USD→q comparison needs two same-date ECB legs, `q-per-EUR / USD-per-EUR`, plus missing-date/quote-kind policy. AdventureWorks recent DEM/FRF/VEB targets remain incompatible with a presumed current-currency lookup; no silent successor conversion or weekend fill. CLDR fractions have DEFAULT/cash-rounding behavior. Currency/date → territory assignments can multiply rows; never aggregate those duplicates as separate rates. SIX rights and withdrawal-month versus tender-date distinctions remain open.

Date-only, local timestamp, UTC instant, service day, interval and duration remain separate. Chinook InvoiceDate is explicitly modelled as date, observed 2021–2025; timezone is not inferred. Employees `9999-01-01` marks open-ended validity. Shift time lacks a date. IANA rules and territory-to-zone relations cannot be replaced by fixed offsets or one zone per country. GOV.UK bank-holiday events are division-specific and the inspected 2019–2028 coverage misses Northwind 2016–2018 and Pubs 1992–1994. UK alone cannot select a division.

Narrow AdventureWorks raw padded bridges accept `KG ` / Kilogram and `LB ` / US pound to CLDR48 mass definitions. A product Weight conversion still needs the matching unit field and pinned decimal factor; BOX/BTL/packaging quantities, GAL and OZ are not guessed. A unit code is not a measurement, and ordinal credit/film/review ratings are not interchangeable physical quantities. Broad measurement/rating and tax-jurisdiction/rate demand groups are rejected as single meanings while individual useful fields remain indexed.

Language identity differs from BCP47 tag, locale, culture, codec and Internet media type. The six padded language-only culture codes have a narrow language-component bridge; invariant whitespace and `zh-cht` remain unresolved. This is not full locale equivalence. Sakila language names need a reviewed name-to-language bridge. Chinook media labels are not automatically IANA type/subtype tokens. Publication, edition, work, file and local title ID remain separate; no ISBN/DOI mapping is inferred. Composer/actor text may be multi-valued; names never become real-person identity.

## Identity, mobility and observations

Reuse core person/customer/employee/organization only with actual dataset-local entity roles. AdventureWorks BusinessEntity may stand for different party kinds; store/site/customer role does not prove a company or legal entity. ROR identifiers concern research organisations; LEI concerns legal-entity records. Their source relationships are useful but no universal ROR↔LEI or fictional-demo-name bridge is accepted. Registration number needs issuer/jurisdiction, parent relation needs type/time, and GLEIF accounting consolidation is not a complete ownership graph. [W3C ORG](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-org/) supports keeping units, memberships, roles, posts and sites distinct.

NaPTAN ATCO, NaptanCode, NPTG locality, ORR NLC/TLC, GTFS IDs and observing-station namespaces remain separate. NaPTAN→NPTG accepts the explicit locality reference, with 1,679/1,679 matches in the dated area010 probe; the literal admin code `009` is not filter `010`. Five GTFS references are accepted within the Allens snapshot only. Route, scheduled trip, location type, calendar and stop-time sequence remain intact. [GTFS](https://gtfs.org/documentation/schedule/reference/) service-day time may exceed 24 hours. The observed feed's 2026–27 dates cannot explain Chinook's 2021–25 invoices; rights remain independently blocked.

EA reading→measure→station accepts exact resource references and UTC observation timestamps. A whole readings.value→water-stage mapping is rejected: select the measure type/unit/datum first. Negative stage is valid. POWER date-keyed objects need an explicit unpivot, fill `-999` handling and UTC daily support before scalar temperature/precipitation mappings. Grid weather, physical stations, travel stops and spatially nearby assets are associations, not shared identity or evidence of causation/flood risk. Ember country/aggregate categories and overlapping generation/source totals require projections before country and energy joins. Annual/monthly comparison needs complete comparable periods, not missing=zero.

CISA→NVD accepts exact CVE identity with source revision and analysis provenance, not product-name matching or inference from KEV absence. IANA tcp/udp→TCP/UDP is an explicit restricted token bridge. Port assignments need transport+port and range handling; HTTP/IP reserved ranges and media-template completeness remain unresolved. SPDX ID semantics do not grant reuse permission to a dataset. These remain honest user-schema opportunities even with little six-demo demand.
