## Hubs, useful distinctions and false joins

**Geography.** Coding-scheme country/area, administrative subdivision, statistical grouping and legal jurisdiction differ. [ISO 3166](https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html) includes dependencies/former codes; [M49](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/) supports statistical groupings, not political affiliation. Country does not prove sovereignty. Raw Region can be sales assignment. Locality, municipality, postal locality and transport place need scope. [UPU S42](https://www.upu.int/en/postal-solutions/programmes-services/addressing-solutions) supports country address templates, not an open global postcode crosswalk. Stable spatial feature identity differs from geometry, CRS and historical boundary version. Postcode associations can be many-to-many.

**Organisations.** [W3C ORG](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-org/) motivates broad organisation, recognised organisation, unit, site, membership and post; [GLEIF LEI](https://www.gleif.org/en/organizational-identity/lei-vlei/the-legal-entity-identifier-lei) concerns legalentity identity. Registration numbers need issuing authority; legal forms need jurisdiction. Supplier/customer/publisher/employer/operator are roles. Parent relations require type, period and exceptions. A trading name, brand or site is not a legalentity key. Synthetic companies may have no real counterpart. A useful vendor-network scenario requires actual portable identifiers or an evidenced crosswalk.

**Commerce/classifications.** [GS1 architecture](https://www.gs1.org/standards/gs1-system-architecture-document/90) distinguishes item class/instance/batch, party/location, logistic unit and shipment. Local SKU is not GTIN; instance needs qualifiers. [UNSD classifications/correspondences](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/econ/) and [ISCO](https://isco.ilo.org/en/isco-08/) motivate distinct activities, products and occupations. Assignments need evidence; scheme/version codes can split/merge many-to-many. Unit, quantity kind, packaging and currency differ. Currency-country is not one-to-one; FX needs directed pair, period, provider and rate type. Local titles/categories do not automatically inherit statistical codes.

**Calendar/time.** [OWL Time](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl-time/) supports instants, intervals and durations; [tz](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tz-link.html) provides local-time rule histories. Event date, validity interval, observation period, jurisdictional observance and transit service day differ. Country alone cannot determine timezone, midnight cannot universally establish date-only semantics and year-9999 can be a sentinel. Historical jurisdiction-specific observance data remains a source gap.

**Mobility/environment.** [GTFS](https://gtfs.org/documentation/schedule/reference/) distinguishes stop location types/routes/trips/services with feed context. [UN/LOCODE](https://unlocode.unece.org/recommendation16/) concerns transport places rather than every individual facility. [WIGOS](https://community.wmo.int/site/knowledge-hub/programmes-and-initiatives/wmo-integrated-global-observing-system-wigos/implementation-of-wmo-integrated-global-observing-system-wigos/wigos-station-identifiers) identifies observing platforms, not passenger stations. Scheduled supply does not measure usage. Weather associations need spatial/temporal support and station relocation history; correlations do not establish causes.

**Aggregate observations.** [OGC OMS](https://www.ogc.org/standards/om/) separates property/feature/procedure/sample/result; [Data Cube](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-data-cube/) supports dimensions/attributes/measures. Population needs area/version/period/residence or cohort definition. Health rates need denominator, age/sex/methodology. Energy product, flow, power, energy and unit differ. Regional health context cannot establish personal health; regional energy mix does not measure site emissions. Observation quality and geographic support must survive enrichment.

**Technology/content.** Language identity differs from BCP47 tag and locale; media type differs from codec/extension/genre/work. URI resource, DNS delegation and organisation identity differ. ccTLD does not prove customer residence. Portable identifiers for researchers, research organisations and publication objects create another potential bridge; source namespaces and scope remain essential. Telephone numbering prefix identifies numbering-plan context, not current residence or personal identity; IP allocation does not prove endpoint location.
